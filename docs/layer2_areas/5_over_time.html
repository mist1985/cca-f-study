⏳ Layer 2 · Area 5: Staying Reliable Over Time (15%)

📍 Map: Whole picture → 5. Over time = keeping it all safe as it runs for hours and grows

In one sentence

Convenient summaries hide problems. Keep the exact, sourced, structured version.

4 ways problems hide

 A summary       → blurs exact numbers     → keep a facts block
 An average      → hides a weak area       → measure each group
 A vague error   → hides what broke        → report it in detail
 A long context  → loses the middle        → key facts first, helpers, files
