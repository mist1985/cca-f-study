5.5 Human review & confidence

📍 Map: Whole picture → 5. Over time → 5.5 Human checks · ⏱ 3 min

The one rule

An average hides weak spots. Check accuracy per document type and per field before cutting human review.

Picture it

"97% accurate overall!" Inside that: handwritten receipts are 60%. Switch off human checks and those errors go straight through.

Know these 🎯

  • Measure accuracy by document type AND by field on a labeled set.
  • Stratified random sampling, including high-confidence items → catches new error patterns where nobody is looking.
  • Field-level confidence from the model → calibrate the thresholds on labeled validation data.
  • Send to humans: low confidence or ambiguous/contradictory sources. Use limited reviewer time where it counts.

👀 What it looks like

Accuracy on 500 labeled documents
                    vendor  total  date   tax_id
typed invoices       99%    99%    98%    97%    ✅ can reduce review
PDF scans            97%    95%    96%    90%    ⚠️ keep sampling
handwritten          88%    61%    70%    55%    ❌ keep human review
overall: 97%  ← hides the bottom row

Piece by piece

  • Break accuracy down by type × field. The overall number hides the bad row.
  • Thresholds for routing are set per field, from labeled data.
  • Keep a random sample of the automated (high-confidence) rows going to humans.
