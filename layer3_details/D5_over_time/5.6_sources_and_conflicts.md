# 5.6 · Sources & conflicting facts (provenance)

📍 **Map:** Whole picture → 5. Over time → **5.6 Sources**  ·  ⏱ 3 min

## The one rule
> Every claim **keeps its source**. Conflicting numbers → **show both, with source and date.**

## Picture it
Five reports become one. Every sentence should still point to where it came from, so anyone can check it.

## Know these 🎯
- Summarizing **loses the claim → source link** unless you keep it structured.
- Helpers output **claim-source mappings**: URL, document name, relevant excerpt. Synthesis **keeps and merges** them.
- Two credible sources disagree → **include both, annotated**. Let the lead decide how to reconcile.
- **Always include dates** (publication or collection). Different dates can explain a "contradiction".
- Report sections: **well-established** vs **contested** findings.
- Format by content: **financial → tables**, **news → prose**, **technical → lists**. Not one format for everything.

## ⚠️ Exam traps
| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
|---|---|
| Pick the more reputable number | Show both, attributed |
| Drop the dates to save space | Dates prevent false contradictions |
| Everything as bullet points | Format by content type |

<details><summary>🔬 Bonus (beyond the official guide)</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>🧠 Say it out loud: two reports give different market sizes. What do you do?</summary>

Keep both with their source and date, mark it as a conflict, and let the lead reconcile.
</details>
