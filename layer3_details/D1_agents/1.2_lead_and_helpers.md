# 1.2 · Lead + helpers (coordinator & subagents)

📍 **Map:** Whole picture → 1. Agents → **1.2 Lead + helpers**  ·  ⏱ 3 min

## The one rule
> **Hub and spoke.** Helpers are isolated. Everything goes through the lead.

## Picture it
A team lead with specialists. Each specialist works in their own room with a fresh notepad. They don't talk to each other. The lead sees every message and every problem.

## Know these 🎯
- Lead's job: split → hand out → collect → combine.
- Lead **picks which helpers to use** based on the question. It doesn't always run the full pipeline.
- **Refinement loop:** lead checks the combined result for gaps → sends targeted work again → combines again.
- Split the scope so helpers **don't overlap** (different subtopics or source types each).
- Why everything goes through the lead: **observability, consistent error handling, controlled information flow.**
- ⚠️ **Too-narrow splitting = missing coverage.** "Creative industries" split into 3 *visual-art* topics → music and film never get researched.

## ⚠️ Exam traps
| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
|---|---|
| Report misses whole topics → blame the search helper | Lead's split was too narrow (check the lead's logs) |
| Helpers talk directly | Everything goes through the lead |
| Always run every helper | Lead picks helpers by complexity |

<details><summary>🔬 Bonus (beyond the official guide)</summary>

Runtime caps: depth 3, 20 at once, budget cap. Cost: agent ~4× chat tokens, team ~15×.
</details>

<details><summary>🧠 Say it out loud: every helper did its job well, but the report misses half the topic. Why?</summary>

The lead split the topic too narrowly. The problem is *what* the helpers were given, not how they did it.
</details>
