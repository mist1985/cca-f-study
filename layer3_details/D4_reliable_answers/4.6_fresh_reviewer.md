# 4.6 · Fresh reviewer, multiple passes

📍 **Map:** Whole picture → 4. Answers → **4.6 Fresh reviewer**  ·  ⏱ 3 min

## The one rule
> The writer is a biased reviewer. Use a **second, independent Claude** without the writer's reasoning.

## Picture it
Proofreading your own essay right after writing it, you read what you *meant*, not what you *wrote*.

## Know these 🎯
- Same session = keeps its own reasoning → **less likely to question its decisions**.
- An independent instance beats "**review yourself**" **and** beats **extended thinking**.
- Big reviews: **per-file passes** (local issues) + **cross-file integration pass** (data flow).
- Extra pass: Claude reports **confidence per finding** → use it to **route** findings for review (after calibration).

## ⚠️ Exam traps
| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
|---|---|
| "Now carefully review your own code" | New independent instance |
| Turn on extended thinking instead | Same context, same bias |
| One giant pass over 30 files | Per-file + integration |

<details><summary>🧠 Say it out loud: why doesn't more thinking fix self-review?</summary>

It's still the same context with the same bias. A fresh instance doesn't have it.
</details>
